Clicada Software Private Limited ("Clicada", "we", "our", or "us") operates mobile applications and software products. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and disclosed when using our applications and services.

Depending on the application and features used, we may collect:

Our storage management applications require access to files and folders on your device in order to provide file browsing, organization, deletion, copying, moving, and storage management features.
